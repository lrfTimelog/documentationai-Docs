---
title: "Sync to a data warehouse"
description: "Replicate TimeLog data, especially time registrations, into a data warehouse with consistent results and light API usage."
---

The Reporting API can copy most of TimeLog's data into a data warehouse or a similar store for further processing.
This guide covers the schedule that keeps the copy consistent without heavy API use.

## Why not re-read everything every night

Time registrations keep changing after the day they are registered: people correct their timesheets, managers approve
them, and invoicing changes their status. A copy that reads only "yesterday" misses those changes. A copy that reads
the full history every night puts unnecessary load on the API, and most methods return all matching records in one
response.

The answer is to read **recent periods often and the full history rarely**.

## Recommended schedule

| How often | What to read again |
|---|---|
| Every day | The last 7 days |
| Every week | The last 4 weeks |
| Every month | The last 3 months |
| Every quarter | The full data set, in batches of one month |

Each run **replaces** the records of its period in your copy. Adjust the periods to how disciplined your organisation
is about registering and approving time: if timesheets are often corrected weeks later, widen the weekly window.

<Callout kind="tip">For time registrations, use [GetWorkUnitsRawPaged](/reporting/methods/get-work-units-raw-paged)
with `startDate` and `endDate` set to the period, and read it page by page. Without a date range the call returns
every registration of the account.</Callout>

## One period, step by step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the period" icon="calendar">
    For example the last 7 days: `startDate` 7 days ago, `endDate` today, both as `yyyy-MM-dd`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read every page" icon="layers">
    Call the method with `page` = `1` and a `pageSize` that fits your environment, then the next page, until a page
    comes back empty.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Replace the period in your copy" icon="refresh-cw">
    Delete your copy's registrations for the period and insert what you read. This also removes registrations that were
    deleted in TimeLog.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Look-up data, such as customers, projects and employees, is much smaller. Read it in full on each run with the
matching raw extract or short list.

## Protect the copy

No TimeLog user's permissions are applied in the Reporting API: the copy holds the whole account's data, including
personal data. Apply your own access rules in the data warehouse and in any reporting tool you connect to it.

## Usage

There are no published hard limits, but heavy, repeated full reads affect other customers. See
[Usage and change policy](/policies).
