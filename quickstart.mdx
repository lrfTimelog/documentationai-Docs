## Get started in under 5 minutes

TimeLog makes time tracking simple and powerful. Follow these steps to create your account, access the dashboard, set up your first project, and log an initial time entry. No credit card required to begin.

## Prerequisites

<Callout kind="info">

Before starting, ensure you have:
- A modern web browser like Chrome or Firefox
- A valid email address for account verification
- About 5 minutes of time

</Callout>

## Account setup and first time entry

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create and verify your account" icon="user-plus">

    Visit `https://app.timelog.com/signup` and fill out the registration form with your email, name, and company details.

    Check your inbox (and spam folder) for a verification email from `noreply@timelog.com`. Click the verification link to activate your account.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Access the TimeLog dashboard" icon="layout-dashboard">

    Return to `https://app.timelog.com/login` and sign in with your verified credentials.

    You'll land on the main dashboard, showing an overview of projects, recent time entries, and quick actions.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Set up your first project" icon="folder-plus">

    Click the `New Project` button in the top-right corner.

    Enter details:
    - Project name: `My First Project`
    - Client: `Internal` (or your first client)
    - Description: `Initial project for time tracking setup`
    - Budget: Leave blank for now

    Click `Create Project` to save.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Log your initial time entry" icon="clock">

    Navigate to your new project by clicking it from the dashboard.

    Select `Log Time` from the project menu.

    Fill in:
    - Date: Today
    - Duration: `1h 30m`
    - Description: `Set up TimeLog account and first project`
    - Task: `Onboarding`

    Click `Save` to record your entry. View it in the project timeline.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="success">
  Success! You've completed your first time entry. TimeLog automatically calculates billable hours and provides insights.
</Callout>

## Integrate via API (optional)

For programmatic time logging, use TimeLog's REST API.

<CodeGroup tabs="JavaScript,cURL">
  ```javascript
  const response = await fetch('https://api.example.com/v1/time-entries', {
    method: 'POST',
    headers: {
      'Authorization': 'Bearer YOUR_API_TOKEN',
      'Content-Type': 'application/json'
    },
    body: JSON.stringify({
      projectId: 'proj_123',
      duration: 5400, // seconds
      description: 'API test entry'
    })
  });
  ```
  ```bash
  curl -X POST https://api.example.com/v1/time-entries \
    -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_TOKEN" \
    -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
    -d '{
      "projectId": "proj_123",
      "duration": 5400,
      "description": "API test entry"
    }'
  ```
</CodeGroup>

<ParamField path="projectId" param-type="string" required="true">
  The ID of the project from your dashboard.
</ParamField>

<ParamField header="Authorization" param-type="string" required="true">
  Bearer token obtained from account settings.
</ParamField>
