## Overview

Turn tracked time into a professional invoice in a few clicks.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Invoices">
    In the sidebar, click on "Invoices".
  </Step>
  <Step title="New Invoice">
    Click the "Create Invoice" button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Time Entries">
    Choose the projects and time entries you want to bill.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill Invoice Details">
    Add client, dates, and any custom notes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save & Send">
    Review, then click "Send" or "Download" to share with your client.
  </Step>
</Steps>
