## Overview

Timelog makes tracking your hours simple.

## Steps

1. Sign in to your Timelog account.
2. On the dashboard, click "Start Timer".
3. Select the project and add a description.
4. Click "Pause" or "Stop" when done.
5. To add time manually, click "Add Entry"
6. Enter project, duration, and details.
7. Save your entry.

## Tip

You can edit or delete entries in the Timesheet view.
