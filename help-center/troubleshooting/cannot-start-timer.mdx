---
title: Cannot Start Timer
description: Resolve issues when the timer doesn't start.
---

## Symptoms

- Timer button is unresponsive
- Nothing happens when you click "Start Timer"

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Refresh page">
    ````
    Hold Shift and click Reload in your browser.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Project Selection">
    Ensure a project is selected before starting the timer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear cache and cookies">
    Log out, clear your browser cache, then sign in again.
  </Step>
</Steps>
