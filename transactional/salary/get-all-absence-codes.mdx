## How it works

The account's absence codes: the reasons absence time is registered on. Each entry carries its code and name, the payroll mapping, the salary account it posts to with factor and sign, how absence is entered, and the approval, capacity and visibility flags. REST's absence-code list returns only the caller's salary group. Configuration only: no employee data.
