## How it works

For the legal entity legalEntityId and the customer customerId, the server takes the requested priceGroupIds that are STANDARD price groups of that legal entity and, for each one, creates a customer-specific price group with the same name and currency if the customer has none with that ID or name, or re-activates the customer's same-named price group if it exists but is inactive - and with it the customer's previously deactivated hourly rates in that group. IDs that are not standard groups of the legal entity are SILENTLY IGNORED; if none match, nothing is created and the call still succeeds. The response does not describe what was created: Return[0] carries the legal entity's name, integer ID and active flag, with IsSystem false. Use GetPriceGroups or the hourly-rate operations to see the result.
