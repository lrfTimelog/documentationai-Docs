## How it works

A pure READ despite the 'New' in its name: nothing is persisted. Same mechanism as GetNewStandardHourlyRates, but for customer customerId: the templates' Details.PriceGroupId is the CUSTOMER's copy of each price group. If the customer has no customer price list in this legal entity, the call fails with 'Customer id: &lt;id> price list not found, use CreateCustomerPriceList to create a customer price list.' Same template caveats as the standard variant: Name and Details.ServiceId belong to an existing service - ignore them; CustomerID on the template is 0 although the price group is the customer's.

## Errors

All errors come back with **HTTP 200**: check `ResponseState`, `ErrorCode` and `Messages`. The errors every operation can return (expired or invalid token, API not enabled, HTTPS required, general errors) are listed once in [Responses and errors](/transactional/guides/responses-and-errors#error-codes).

Errors specific to this operation:

| ResponseState | ErrorCode | Message | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Error | 40001 | Error during call on TimeLog.API, Message 'Customer id: \{customerID\} price list not found, use CreateCustomerPriceList to create a customer price list.' | — | Create the customer's price groups with CreateCustomerPriceList, then call again. |
