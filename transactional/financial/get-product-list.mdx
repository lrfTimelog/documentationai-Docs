## How it works

No paging. Each product costs two extra look-ups; if any product's currency or product number does not resolve, the WHOLE call fails.

## Errors

All errors come back with **HTTP 200**: check `ResponseState`, `ErrorCode` and `Messages`. The errors every operation can return (expired or invalid token, API not enabled, HTTPS required, general errors) are listed once in [Responses and errors](/transactional/guides/responses-and-errors#error-codes).

Errors specific to this operation:

| ResponseState | ErrorCode | Message | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Error | 40001 | Error during call on TimeLog.API, Message 'Unable to retrieve result from … with ID:\{currencyID\}' | Any returned product has a currency ID that is not a currency - the whole list is lost. | Send a valid CostCurrencyID; repair the product to restore GetProductList. |
