## How it works

Looks up the legal entity by its GUID and returns its ACTIVE price groups: the standard groups AND every customer-specific copy, with nothing on the contract that tells them apart - customer copies carry the standard group's name and IsDefault = false. No customer filter exists. Order is not defined. An unknown or all-zero GUID is an error with a raw null-reference text, not an empty list.
