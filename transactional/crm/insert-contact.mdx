## How it works

Creates a contact on the customer given by Contact.CustomerGUID. Firstname, Lastname, Address.Country and CustomerGUID are required. On success Return[0] is the stored contact and ResponseState is Success. Custom fields and external keys sent with the contact are saved too. Validation and privilege failures come back as ResponseState Invalid with the reason in Messages and - unusually - the contact you sent in Return[0]: check ResponseState, not Return, to know whether anything was created. Retry safety: supply your own GUID.

## Errors

All errors come back with **HTTP 200**: check `ResponseState`, `ErrorCode` and `Messages`. The errors every operation can return (expired or invalid token, API not enabled, HTTPS required, general errors) are listed once in [Responses and errors](/transactional/guides/responses-and-errors#error-codes).

Errors specific to this operation:

| ResponseState | ErrorCode | Message | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Invalid | 10002 | \{field\}, was null or empty | Firstname, Lastname or Address.Country empty. | Send the named value. |
| Invalid | 30017 | Unknown Country ISO Code: \{code\} | Address.Country is not a known ISO code. | — |
| Invalid | 10005 | \{description\}, should not be a 0-GUID | CustomerGUID all zeros. | Send the object's GUID. |
| Error | 40001 | Error during call on TimeLog.API, Message 'Unable to retrieve result from … with GUID\{guid\}' | CustomerGUID is not an existing customer. | Check the customer GUID. |
| Invalid | 20012 | User: \{initials\}, has Insufficient Access. Missing privilege was: \{role\} | — | — |
| Invalid | 10001 | Insert of Contact with ID (\{id\}) failed! | — | For updates, read the contact first and send its GUID. |
