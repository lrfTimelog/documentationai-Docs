## How it works

Lists every allocation of the employee identified by `initials` whose TASK is active - regardless of whether the allocation itself is completed. Each item carries the allocation's GUID and integer ID, task, planned hours, contract hourly rate ID, dates and the employee header. Allocations whose rate ID is not a contract hourly rate are silently left out. The list is not paged and has no defined order.
